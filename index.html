WORKING INTERNAL SYSTEM

One System for
Daily Operations

I developed TyOS to make it easier for our team to manage purchase orders, check incoming shipments, and search for product details.

BUSINESS CHALLENGE Purchase orders, shipment updates and product information were difficult to review consistently across separate files and conversations.

MY ROLE Gather workflow requirements, organise information and permissions, implement with AI-assisted coding, test with sample and operational scenarios, then iterate and deploy.

IMPLEMENTATION A central D1-backed system with role-based access, validations, record history, search and R2-hosted product documents.

WORKING SOLUTION Purchase order management, incoming-stock visibility and a searchable product library are implemented. Detailed partial-shipment quantity allocation is clearly marked as planned.

BUSINESS VALUE A shared operational starting point that makes records easier to find, review and follow up.
